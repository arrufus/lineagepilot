# Beta: share how often you use it

The beta ends with a decision on whether LineagePilot is worth carrying on with. It depends on whether teams use the review week after week, so it helps to know how often you do.

**Usage counts are off unless you turn them on.** When they are on, LineagePilot counts, week by week, on this machine:

- the days on which you had a dbt project open with LineagePilot running;
- how many times you opened the Change Impact Review;
- how many times you copied it as Markdown.

That is all. The counts hold nothing about any project, no names, paths or contents, and **LineagePilot never sends them anywhere**: it makes no network connections at all. **Copy Usage Counts** puts them on the clipboard as a small table, for you to read and paste into the weekly check-in issue. Turning counting off deletes them, at once if LineagePilot is running, or else when it next starts.

The setting is `lineagePilot.usageCounts.enabled`. It can only be set in your own user settings, never by a workspace.

To report a bug or send feedback, open an issue in the LineagePilot repository: the beta's issue forms say what is useful to include.
